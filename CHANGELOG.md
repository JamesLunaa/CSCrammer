# Changelog

All notable changes to CSCrammer are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0] - 2026-10-27

First release.

### Added

- 2,500 bundled practice questions across the five CSC exam subjects: Verbal Ability, Numerical Ability, Analytical Ability, General Information, and Philippine Constitution & Public Service Ethics. They load on first launch with nothing to import.
- **Quick review:** 10 random questions, with instant feedback and explanations.
- **Practice:** choose a subject, topics, difficulty and how many questions.
- **Mock exam:**
  - Full (170 items in 3h 10m) or mini (50 items in 55 minutes).
  - A question navigator and flags.
  - Submits itself when time runs out.
  - You need 80% to pass.
- **Results:** score, a per-subject breakdown, and an answer review with a "Wrong only" filter.
- **Review:** re-drill questions you got wrong, plus any you bookmarked.
- **Progress:**
  - overall accuracy
  - accuracy per subject
  - weakest topics
  - daily streak
  - session history
- **Settings:** dark theme (the default), day theme, or follow the system. You can also reset your progress.
- Works fully offline. The app asks for no permissions and needs no account.
- Accessibility support: TalkBack headings and announcements, and layouts that still fit at 200% font size.
